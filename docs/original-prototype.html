How to use

  1. Power drum: click the upper half to add plus, lower half to add minus. Outer zones move in 1.00 D steps, inner zones in 0.25 D steps.
  2. Axis wheel: click left or right of centre to rotate. Outer zones move 10°, middle zones 5°, inner zones 1°.
  3. Rotate the axis wheel until the mires can be focused crisply, then find the two drum powers that clear each line set.
  4. In minus cyl format, the most positive reading is the sphere, the difference between the two readings is the cylinder, and the axis is read from the wheel.
  5. Use Randomise prescription for a new lens, or show the prescription and edit the values directly. The cyl format button transposes between minus and plus cyl.
