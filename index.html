How to use the focimeter

  1. Find the axis first. Turn the axis wheel until the two sets of lines look square and clean rather than skewed. A cylinder viewed off its axis can never be brought sharp, no matter where the drum sits.
  2. Clear the first set of lines. Turn the power drum until one set of lines snaps into focus. Note the reading.
  3. Clear the second set. Keep turning to focus the other set, and note that reading too.
  4. Write the result. In minus-cyl form the sphere is the more positive of the two readings, the cylinder is the second reading minus the sphere, and the axis is whatever the wheel says.

Marking

Sphere and cylinder must be exact to 0.25 D. The axis tolerance depends on the difficulty you pick. A correctly transposed answer is accepted in either cylinder form, and the axis is ignored for a spherical lens.
